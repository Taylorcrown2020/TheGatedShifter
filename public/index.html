What is The Gated Shifter®?

Collector Car Intelligence & Trust

The Gated Shifter® is a collector car intelligence and trust platform built for the analog automotive world. We bring together market intelligence, provenance, significant vehicles, trusted specialists and opportunities in one trusted ecosystem.

The platform is being built to help collectors and industry professionals discover vehicles, understand markets, evaluate provenance, identify trusted expertise and make better-informed decisions across the collector car ecosystem.

The Gated Shifter® is a collector car intelligence platform connecting enthusiasts, collectors and industry professionals with trusted market intelligence, provenance, vehicles, specialists and opportunities — all in one ecosystem.

  • Market IntelligenceWhat comparable cars are actually doing, read across the analog market.
  • ProvenanceHistory, documentation and ownership, evaluated before money moves.
  • Significant VehiclesCars of consequence, and the collections they sit in.
  • Trusted SpecialistsRestorers, historians and marque experts, accredited not advertised.
  • OpportunitiesWhat is quietly available, shown to the people it suits.
