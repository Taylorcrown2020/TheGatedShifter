Privacy

Last updated 8 August 2026

The Gated Shifter is a private ownership platform in development. This page describes what we collect through the Founding Access form and what happens to it.

What we collect

Only what you enter on the form: member type, name, email, mobile, city, marques or specialty, what you are looking for right now, and any notes. We also record how you reached the form — a QR code placement, a tablet at an event, or the open web — and the date and time of your request.

Why we collect it

To reply to your request, to consider you for the Founders Registry, and to introduce you to the collectors, specialists and partners relevant to what you are looking for. Marketing updates are sent only if you check that box.

Who sees it

The Gated Shifter, and the email service that delivers our messages to you. Your details are never sold, rented, or shared with other members without your agreement.

Deleting your information

Every email we send includes a deletion link in the footer. Opening it and confirming removes your record from our database and your contact record from our email system, and sends you one final confirmation. You may sign up again at any time afterwards.

You can also write to private@gatedshifter.co and ask us to delete your information.

How long we keep it

Until you ask us to delete it, or until it is no longer needed to consider or serve your request.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Submissions are written by our own server to a private database that is not reachable from a browser. IP addresses are stored only as a one-way hash, used to limit abuse of the form.
